## Direction contract

THESIS: This page is the operator's guide that shipped in the box with a workstation —
the spiral-bound manual with a part number on the cover, numbered procedures, figure
captions and CAUTION boxes. It refuses the category default the last build landed on: a
cool-grey systems page with mono eyebrows and same-size feature cards. A manual is the one
form where "here is exactly what this does, here is what will go wrong" is the native
voice rather than an apology, which is what this product's honesty needs.

OWN-WORLD: A committed oxblood field (#8C1D2C) carrying the masthead and the section
tabs, printed on neutral warm paper (#EDEBE6) in near-black ink (#1A1714), hairlines at
#C9C4BA. Archivo for headings and furniture, Source Serif 4 for body, JetBrains Mono only
for commands and measured values. Furniture is the component language: hanging numerals on
procedures, figures numbered by section with captions beneath, tables ruled top and bottom
only, a CAUTION box with a bold label and a rule, a part number and revision in the corner.
No cards, no eyebrows, no icon-heading-text triples.

STORY: A developer whose laptop has become the bottleneck understands in one screen that
this provisions a real machine and hands it to the agent they already use; believes it
because the page shows the actual app with a live machine and states its own rough edges
plainly; and runs the brew command.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Oxblood masthead band across the full width, title "Codex Remote" set
large in Archivo with "Operator's Guide" beneath it and a part-number line in mono at the
right edge. Directly under, on paper: the proposition in two lines of serif, then the
install command as procedure step 1 in a ruled block with a copy control. Right of it at
desktop width, Figure 1-1 — the real screenshot of the app with a live machine and its
caption. Primary action sits above the fold on both widths.

FORM: Vintage workstation operator's manual (DEC/Sun era). Candidate 7 of my grounded
list; assigned by the roll, seed key a53fbdcd.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
